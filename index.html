Our Farm

A walk in the woods. A tree worth finding.

We look forward to welcoming customers old and new to walk through the woods in search of their tree. Our farm sits on the Niagara Escarpment, among mature woodland, meadows and quiet trails.

The selection is somewhat limited, but if your tree is going in a corner or on a wall, you should be able to find something special and unique.

Our Story

A place of peace.

Mark’s Christmas Trees is a place of peace.

Since 1995

Caretakers of the land.

Since 1995, we have been caretakers of this land and protectors of the watershed system, as our pond feeds into Spencer Creek.

No chemicals or pesticides are utilized to control invasive wild grape vines that take hold of any trees that they encounter. We do our best to eradicate them by hand, but other than that, our trees are allowed to grow as nature intended, and allow you to bring a bit of unspoiled nature into your home.

“Each tree is unique, and perfectly imperfect, just like us.”

And when we are all dressed up, we all look beautiful.

Perfectly imperfect

Trees are like people.

With the right attention and love, their best features shine. Your eyes will spot a tree that tucks in perfectly in the corner, or one that is just right for the wall. A tree that is less full on one side won’t gobble up the room. A round one would be great in front of the window.

Keep an open mind. The right tree is often not the one you expected.

An old-fashioned experience

A walk through nature.

Exploring the property is an unspoiled walk through nature, and an opportunity to experience something profoundly simple and pure with the ones you love. What better way to keep the Christmas spirit alive while creating traditions and memories which will not only last, but hopefully carry on.

It takes you back to a time when getting your tree was a family adventure to cherish. Take it home, decorate it with love, and it becomes your special tree, a symbol of love, joy and beautiful memories for the future.

Welcome back

It is a privilege.

It is a privilege for us to welcome and visit with customers old and new, to see families grow and grow up before our eyes, and to share in the joy of the season.

A note on behalf of our trees

Large trees take decades to grow and benefit the environment. Some are stressed by weather and invasive vines causing the lower section to struggle while the top of the tree thrives. We encourage you to top these trees, but please be sure before cutting it down, that you intend to take the good part in its entirety.

What to bring

Warm, sturdy footwear. The ground is rocky, with stumps and invasive vines, so walk mindfully.

Your own saw if possible. We can lend you one.

Rope, straps or a tarp. We can wrap your tree for $5.00.

On the farm

The barn is unheated but sheltered, with benches, tables and chairs.

No public toilets, but if nature calls, you're welcome in our home.

Access

Paths are fine for strollers; the fields are trickier. We have a few sleds or you can bring your own.

Wheelchair access on driveway and around the house is manageable, but would be difficult on the walking paths, especially when there is snow.

We are dog friendly

Dogs are welcome on a leash.

Visiting with Kids

Know what to expect, so everyone stays happy.

This is a natural woodland farm, not a theme park. A little planning goes a long way with little ones.

1

Park for free. There is room for vans and larger vehicles.

2

Walk the woods together. Take your time. There is no rush, and the owners are happy to chat.

3

Choose your tree. Let the kids help pick. Every tree is a little different.

4

An adult cuts it. Please bring a saw or borrow ours. Saws are for adults only.

5

We wrap it for $5.00. Then pay by cash or e-transfer, and tie it on with your rope or straps.

Little legs

Warming up

The barn is unheated but sheltered, with benches, tables and chairs for a short break.
